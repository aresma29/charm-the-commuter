# Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't

A one-line weather forecast for Brindlewick — a city that doesn't exist, for commuters who wish it were real. Light drizzle, a 40% chance of existential dread, and the 8:04 train on time for once.

## How it works

`main.py` resolves `entry.txt` relative to its own location (via `pathlib.Path(__file__).resolve().parent`), reads the single forecast line, strips the trailing newline, prints it, and exits 0. Stdlib only — no arguments, no network, no side effects.
